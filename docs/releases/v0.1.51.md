Scorpion 0.1.51 for Windows and macOS: the orb can now be a holographic humanoid. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.51.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.51-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.51.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.50

- **The orb humanoid** (**Settings → General → Orb look**): a 3D hologram figure drawn in your theme colour, with warm light in the opposite colour inside its face. Its head bends smoothly on its neck, follows your hand, pointer and face, nods and shakes, and answers your voice. A constellation of stars joined by glowing veins fills the screen behind it.
- **The constellation launcher is gone.** Its settings move over: your summon shortcut is now the orb shortcut (default Super+Space).

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
