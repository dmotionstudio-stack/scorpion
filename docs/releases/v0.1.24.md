Scorpion 0.1.24 for Windows and macOS: the orb becomes the system, with its own taskbar, a tidier chat, and a new small model. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.24.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.24-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.24.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.23

- **The orb is the system (Windows).** Every app you open while the orb is open gets a tile, up to six at once, side by side like Windows Snap. Drag the bar between tiles to resize, move a tile with its arrows or by dragging its title bar onto another. The orb keeps a tile of its own with no frame around it, and moves to the room the apps leave: an app that opened on the left goes left, one on the right goes right.
- **Home, the chat and Settings open as a tile beside the orb**, so the orb is always on the screen. The tile's ✕, or Esc, closes the page.
- **Scorpion's taskbar inside the orb's screen**, with everything the desktop one has: Scorpion, the intent wheel, Apps and Files, pinned apps and open windows (a click puts a window in a tile), drives, network, battery, notifications, mail, chat, volume, the clock, Settings and power. Customize it as before (right-click it): on any edge (the edge rail by default, or a bar), drag it to another edge, drag its inner edge to resize it, hide it automatically, names, centered, size, look.
- **The orb's screen is just the orb**, a typing box with Talk, and the taskbar. The bottom settings bar, the bubbles around the orb, Minimize and Exit are gone; their controls (wake word, hand gestures, face tracking, zoom, the voice chat switch, orb look) are in **Settings → General → Orb**. The floating orb window from 0.1.22 is gone.
- **Chat: one drop-down for Chat, Plan, Build and Auto-approve** in the message box (Auto-approve left the top bar). The Preview, Terminal and Plan panels each have an edge to drag, the conversation always keeps its room, and in a narrow window the panels go under it. With the sidebar closed, the top bar no longer sits under its button.
- **LFM3 1.7B** (Liquid AI, 1.1 GB) is in the model catalog: a small multilingual model (16 languages, not Haitian Creole), quick with Thinking off. It needs the new engine below.
- **A newer model engine** (llama.cpp v0.9.0), which also runs newer model families. A reply's thinking room now counts on top of its answer, so a model that thinks at length is no longer cut off in the middle of an answer.
- **Other models in the catalog**: Gemma 4 E4B, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 9B, MiMo V2.6, Nemotron 3.5 Lightning and EmbeddingGemma 2, downloaded in the app. (0.1.23 lists them too.)

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release; its new ISO has the same orb, LFM3 and EmbeddingGemma built in.
