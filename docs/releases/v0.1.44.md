Scorpion 0.1.44 for Windows and macOS: old chats no longer jump into the chat you are in. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.44.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.44-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.44.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.43

- **The chat you are in comes first.** Scorpion used to attach pieces of other chats to your message when the words matched, so a short "continue" could send the model back to an old chat. Now, in a chat that already has turns, nothing from other chats is attached unless you point at the past ("last time", "you said", "the other chat", "where we left off"). In a new chat, a bare "continue" or "yes" pulls nothing in either, and anything that is attached is labelled as coming from other chats. You can still ask Scorpion to look through your past chats yourself.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
