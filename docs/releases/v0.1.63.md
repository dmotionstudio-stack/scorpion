Scorpion 0.1.63 for Windows and Mac: throw a closed hand at the screen to swap the orb and the humanoid. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.63.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.63-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.63.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.62

- **Throw a closed hand to swap the orb and the humanoid.** Close your hand and throw it forward, toward the screen, and the orb turns into the humanoid, or the humanoid back into the orb, in the same slow transformation as the voice command. Do it as many times as you like. A slow move, a fist swung sideways, an open hand pushed forward (still "Back") and a held fist (still pauses the hands) do not count.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
