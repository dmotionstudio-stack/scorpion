Scorpion 0.1.60 for Windows and macOS: Scorpion waits until you have finished talking, and listens for your voice, not the room. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.59

- **It waits until you are done.** A pause to think no longer ends your turn. Scorpion now waits at least a second and a half of quiet, longer the longer you have been speaking, and longer again when your sentence trails off ("and", "because", a comma). Before, a pause of well under a second was enough for it to start answering.
- **Voice focus.** Listening starts on a human voice and goes on only while there is one: fans, keyboards, hiss and hum no longer start a turn or hold one open. It is on by default: say **"block background noises"** or **"stop blocking background noise"** to change it.
- **Ask what the room sounds like.** Say **"what's that noise?"** or **"identify the background noises"** and Scorpion listens to the room for a few seconds and tells you what it hears (voices, a hum, clicks, hiss, music). It is the only time it listens to the room itself, and nothing is kept.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
