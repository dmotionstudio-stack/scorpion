Scorpion 0.1.32 for Windows and macOS: an even faster orb. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.32.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.32-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.32.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.31

- **The orb answers sooner.** Its prompt and tools are read ahead of time when it opens. A short pause already ends a short question (0.6 s) and starts the transcription, so the words are ready when you really stop. When your chat model is a big one (over 3.2 GB) the orb answers with a smaller, faster installed model (Qwen3.5 4B, LFM3 1.7B); **Settings → General → Orb** says which, and **Settings → Voice → Voice model → Same as chat** keeps your chat model.
- **Things done at once, without the model (English):** the time and date, volume up, down and mute, "open Safari" (any installed app), a screenshot, dark or light mode, and the battery.
- **When an answer takes a while,** the orb shows "Working on it…" after 1.2 s and says "One moment." after 3 s.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
