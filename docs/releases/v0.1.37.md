Scorpion 0.1.37 for Windows and macOS: reasoning that stays open and a view of what Scorpion does in the background. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.37.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.37-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.37.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.36

- **Reasoning stays open.** In the chat, the model's reasoning is never folded away: it is open while it is written and open after the answer ("Thought for 12 s"), before and after each step, in helpers' work too. Click a title to close one; it stays closed.
- **See what it is doing in the background.** The line under an answer now says what Scorpion is busy with before the first word: loading the model, remembering earlier chats, searching your documents, summarizing a long chat, reading your message and its tools. A tool at work is open, with what it was asked to do.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
