Scorpion 0.1.50 for Windows and macOS: every app keeps its own window buttons in the orb. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.50.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.50-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.50.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.49

- **Apps in the orb keep their own minimize, maximize and close buttons.** Scorpion no longer cuts off an app's own title bar when it puts the app in a tile, so every app shows its usual three buttons at its top right (some showed them and some didn't). Minimize tucks the app away, close closes it, and maximize gives it the whole screen as a normal window. The tile's trim button is gone.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
