Scorpion 0.1.49 for Windows and macOS: a faster voice and a simpler taskbar. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.49.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.49-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.49.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.48

- **The Orb talks sooner and without pauses.** Each spoken sentence is now prepared as soon as it arrives instead of after the one before has finished, the silence the voice puts at the start and end of every sentence is trimmed (it added up to nearly a second between sentences), and the sentences are played back to back. **Settings → Voice → Faster voice** (about 310 MB, asked before it downloads) makes the voice about six times quicker to produce on computers where the standard one is slower than speech.
- **The intent wheel is gone**, on Windows, Mac and Scorpion OS: its summon button on the taskbar, Win+Shift+Space (and Super+Space on Scorpion OS), and its settings. The command palette (Ctrl+K) still opens Write a note, Browse, Draw something and Focus timer.
- **A taskbar on the left or right now stacks Home, Apps and Files one under the other** instead of in a row that was cut off.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
