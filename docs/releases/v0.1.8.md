Scorpion 0.1.8 for Windows and macOS. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion.Setup.0.1.8.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.8-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.8.dmg` |

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**). The README's Install section has the details.

## What's new since 0.1.7

**Scorpion as your desktop (Windows)**
- Scorpion can be your Windows desktop or replace the Windows taskbar: a home screen, an edge rail with your open apps, clock and notifications, and Apps and Files inside Scorpion. Switching is only ever done from Settings, and the Windows desktop comes back if anything goes wrong.
- The orb as the system: full mode, apps shown inside the orb, your hand as the mouse, and a constellation map of your projects.

**The summon dot and intent wheel**
- When Scorpion is minimized, its summon dot stays on the desktop. Drag it anywhere and it stays there. Click it, or press Win+Shift+Space anywhere, for the intent wheel.
- A new glass wheel that grows out of the dot over a frosted picture of your screen. Your recent windows sit around it as tilted cards with their names.
- While Scorpion is minimized, the rail can stay down the right edge with your open apps.

**Windows and taskbar (Settings → Windows and taskbar)**
- Apps open full screen (their dialogs keep their size).
- Stage: touch the left edge of the screen and all your open apps slide in as live pictures. Pick one and the window in front steps aside. A window you minimize flies into Stage.
- The taskbar can hide until the pointer touches its edge. Choose what it shows (clock, network, battery, notifications, mail, chat, volume), and add, remove and reorder its apps.

**Everywhere**
- A command palette (Ctrl+K) with everything Scorpion can do, by keyboard, voice or the wheel.
- A friendlier spoken first start, accessibility options and a Performance switch (Full, Balanced, Light).
- One design system across every screen, and a readable Light theme.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
