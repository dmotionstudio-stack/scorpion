Scorpion 0.1.52 for Windows and macOS: the orb humanoid introduces itself, listens in a new colour, and sits bigger on the bottom edge. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.52.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.52-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.52.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.51

- **The humanoid introduces itself.** The first time it appears in a run of Scorpion it nods, says who it is (aloud when the voice is installed) and shows the words under it.
- **A new Listening look.** While it listens the figure turns to the opposite of your theme colour, rings of sound close in toward its head and swell with your voice, and it leans toward you.
- **Bigger, on the bottom edge.** The figure is larger and always sits on the bottom edge of the screen, on any screen, with the constellation of glowing veins behind it.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
