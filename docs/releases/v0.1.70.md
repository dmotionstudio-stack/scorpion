Scorpion 0.1.70 for Windows and Mac: a music player of its own, switches for the camera, Wi-Fi and more by command, Scorpion's browser inside Scorpion, and a 2.5-second pause before it answers. Everything still runs on your computer.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.70.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.70-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.70.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). Macs on 0.1.65 or newer update themselves the same way. If your Mac has an older version, install this one by hand once (open the disk image and drag Scorpion into Applications): it is signed and notarized by Apple, and updates are automatic from then on. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The Windows installer isn't signed with a paid certificate: click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it.

## What's new since 0.1.68 (0.1.69 was not published: its Mac build failed a test of the music player on macOS)

- **Scorpion's own music player.** It plays the music on your computer (the Library and your Music folder) inside Scorpion, with a small player on the screen, in the orb too: drag it by its title. Say or type "play some music", "play Bob Marley", "play some jazz", "pause the music", "next song", "stop the music", "what song is this" or "shuffle on". The agent can do the same, and the music goes quiet while Scorpion listens or speaks. Nothing is streamed.
- **Switches by command.** "Turn off the camera", "turn on Wi-Fi", "Bluetooth off", "airplane mode on", "do not disturb on", "night light off" and "turn off the microphone" work by voice, in the command palette and for the agent. Windows flips camera and microphone access, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, airplane mode and do not disturb; a Mac does Wi-Fi, the microphone level and (with blueutil) Bluetooth; Scorpion OS does Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, airplane mode, the microphone and do not disturb. When a system has no switch for something, Scorpion says so and opens its Settings page. The agent asks first before it turns the camera or microphone back on, or cuts you off (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, airplane mode).
- **Scorpion's browser stays inside Scorpion.** When the agent browses, the pages open in the browser panel beside the chat (in the orb, the chat's tile), never in a window of their own.
- **It answers after 2.5 seconds of quiet.** When you talk with Scorpion in the orb or humanoid, it starts answering once you have paused for 2.5 seconds. Change it (or choose Automatic) in Settings → Voice and hands.
- **Hand gestures and face tracking without showing anything.** Settings → General → Orb has a new switch, "Show the camera view and tracking status on the screen". Turn it off and they keep working with nothing on the screen (an error still shows). By voice: "hide the camera view".

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
