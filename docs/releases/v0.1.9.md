Scorpion 0.1.9 for Windows and macOS: a fix for the taskbar's auto-hide. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion.Setup.0.1.9.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.9-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.9.dmg` |

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**). The README's Install section has the details.

Installing over 0.1.8 keeps your settings, chats and models.

## What's fixed since 0.1.8

- With **Hide automatically** on, the rail and the taskbar now slide away completely. Before, only their icons hid and an empty dark bar stayed on the screen. Now just a thin glowing line is left at the edge; touch it with the pointer to bring the bar back.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
