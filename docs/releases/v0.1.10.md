Scorpion 0.1.10 for Windows and macOS: put the taskbar on any edge of the screen and the summon dot in any corner. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion.Setup.0.1.10.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.10-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.10.dmg` |

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**). The README's Install section has the details.

Installing over 0.1.9 keeps your settings, chats and models.

## What's new since 0.1.9

**The taskbar on any edge**
- Put Scorpion's taskbar along the bottom, top, left or right of the screen: **Settings → Windows and taskbar**, or right-click an empty part of the taskbar → **Position**.
- Along the top or bottom, the rail lays itself out in a row; down a side, the classic bar becomes a column of icons. Auto-hide works on every edge.
- With the taskbar on the left, Stage slides in from the right edge instead.
- When Scorpion is minimized, the rail now stops short of the Windows taskbar instead of covering its corner.

**The summon dot in any corner**
- Choose a corner for the dot in **Settings → Windows and taskbar**, or drag it: drop it near a corner and it settles into that corner. Anywhere else, it stays where you drop it.
- The dot is sized to your screen: bigger on a large screen, smaller on a small one. The intent wheel grows a little on a large screen too.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
