Scorpion 0.1.17 for Windows and macOS: a Thinking switch in the chat, models chosen by how fast your computer is, grouped taskbar icons, USB drives with eject, a date and time setting, and New folder or document from right-click. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.17.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.17-arm64.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.16

**Faster answers on slower computers**
- **Thinking** (Auto, Off, Quick, Normal, Deep) is at the top of every chat and in Settings. Models that think before they answer (Qwen3.5, Qwen3, gpt-oss) spend most of the wait on it; Off skips it, Quick keeps it short. Auto turns it off where the model is slow on your computer.
- **Models show how fast they will be here** (Models page and the model picker): worked out from your computer's memory speed, processor cores and graphics card. They are estimates, marked "about".
- **Automatic model choice.** On a new install Scorpion uses the most capable model that answers comfortably on your computer. Choosing a model yourself keeps it until you pick **Automatic** again. If you already chose a model, nothing changes.
- The model list is now **Qwen3.5 4B** (recommended) and **Qwen3 1.7B** (only for slow computers: fast, but weak with tools and long jobs). Qwen3 4B Instruct, gpt-oss 20B and Nomic Embed are no longer offered; any you already have keep working. Documents (chat with your files) need an embedding model you import yourself.

**Desktop and taskbar**
- **Right-click** on text anywhere in Scorpion gives Undo, Cut, Copy, Paste, Select all, spelling fixes and link actions.
- **New folder or document** from right-click in Files and on the Scorpion OS desktop: folder, text, Word-style document, note, spreadsheet, web page. The Scorpion OS desktop now shows your Desktop folder as icons.
- **Group taskbar icons.** Put pinned apps in your own groups (right-click an app → Group): one icon with a dropdown of its apps. An app's open windows share one icon with a count and a list. Group the status icons (network, battery, notifications, mail, chat, sound) in one dropdown too: right-click the taskbar.
- **USB and external drives** get an icon while one is plugged in, with **Open** and **Eject safely**.
- The summon dot and Stage are gone; the intent wheel opens from the taskbar's summon button or **Win+Shift+Space**.

**Privacy and system**
- **Hand gestures and listening start off.** Turn them on when you want them (G in the orb, the Gestures and Talk buttons), or switch them back on at start in Settings → General → Voice and hands.
- **Date and time** (Settings → General → System): follow the internet's time, sync now, or set it by hand. The system asks for your permission before any change.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
