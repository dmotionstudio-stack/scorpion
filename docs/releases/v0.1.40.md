Scorpion 0.1.40 for Windows and macOS: folders in Files open again, and an app's own minimize, maximize and close buttons are always hidden in its tile. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.39

- **Folders in Files open.** Double-clicking a folder (Downloads, Documents, Pictures…) did nothing, because the first click redrew the list and lost the second. It opens now, and so does pressing **Enter** on the selected item.
- **No more app buttons in a tile.** An app that draws its own title bar no longer shows its own minimize, maximize and close buttons in its tile, and the ▔ button no longer has a setting that shows them: it goes from the corner cut to a taller cut and back. A "none" you had saved counts as the corner cut.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
