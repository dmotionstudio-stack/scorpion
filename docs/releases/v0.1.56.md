Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.55

- **The orb and the humanoid are one.** Say or type **"transform into the humanoid"** or **"show me your humanoid"**, and **"transform into the orb"** or **"show me the orb"** to go back. The switch is a slow transformation: the orb folds into a point of light and the humanoid grows out of it, and the other way round, the humanoid folds into its heart and the orb swells out of that point.
- **The humanoid fits its tile.** When apps are open in the orb, the humanoid now frames its whole figure inside its own tile at the biggest size that fits, instead of being zoomed in and cropped.
- **Eight entrances.** Rise, descend and scan are gone; it enters by growing out from its heart, dissolving, a spiral, a spin-in, a jelly pop, a fly-in, assembling itself, or growing from a seed of light.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
