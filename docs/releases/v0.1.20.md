Scorpion 0.1.20 for Windows and macOS: the orb becomes a voice chat, and the model catalog grows. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.20.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.20-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.20.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.19

- **The orb is a voice chat.** With the voice installed, the orb's screen is just the orb, the captions and Talk: say "Scorpion", ask, and the answer is spoken. No typing box, shortcuts, apps or camera on it, and it listens for its name whenever it is open. Turn it off in **Settings → Voice and hands → The orb** to get the full orb back.
- **More models in the catalog** (**Settings → Models**, downloaded in the app, size and checksum checked): Gemma 4 E4B (5.1 GB), DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 9B (5.2 GB), MiMo V2.6 (5.2 GB), Nemotron 3.5 Lightning (23.7 GB, for powerful computers) and EmbeddingGemma 2 (290 MB, for Chat with documents). Scorpion never picks these by itself; Qwen3.5 4B stays the recommended model.
- The README describes the current models and features.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release. The new ISO has EmbeddingGemma 2 built in beside the two Qwen models.
