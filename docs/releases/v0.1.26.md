Scorpion 0.1.26 for Windows and macOS: ChatGPT, Claude and other apps with their own title bar open inside the orb, and the Home header can be shrunk. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.25

- **Apps with their own title bar open inside the orb (Windows).** ChatGPT, Claude and other Chromium apps (which draw their own title bar and often stay on top of other windows) are framed like any other app. Only the corner with their own minimize, maximize and close buttons is hidden, so the only buttons are Scorpion's. The ▔ button in the tile's title bar changes this (nothing, the buttons, or the whole strip), remembered per app. When an app cannot open inside, Scorpion now says why.
- **Apps bigger than their tile** are cut off at the tile edge instead of spilling over the orb or the taskbar.
- **Home header resizes.** Drag the bar under the header on the Home page to make it smaller and see more tools; double-click the bar to hide or bring back the header.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
