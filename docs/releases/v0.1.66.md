Scorpion 0.1.66 for Windows and Mac: the orb screen speaks your language, a Settings button on the taskbar, and a few fixes. Everything still runs on your computer.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.66.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.66-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.66.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). Macs on 0.1.65 or newer update themselves the same way. If your Mac has an older version, install this one by hand once (open the disk image and drag Scorpion into Applications): it is signed and notarized by Apple, and updates are automatic from then on. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The Windows installer isn't signed with a paid certificate: click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it.

## What's new since 0.1.65

- **The orb screen follows your language.** Choose a language for Scorpion's voice (Settings → Voice) and the orb's own words change with it, in all 38 languages: the status (Listening, Thinking…), the Talk and Send buttons, the typing hint, the keys line at the bottom, and what it says while it works ("Opening Files…") and for your gestures. Scorpion's answers were already in that language. Arabic, Hebrew, Urdu and Persian read right to left.
- **A Settings button on the taskbar.** The Files button on the taskbar (the Windows one and the one inside the orb) is now Settings. Files is still one click away in Apps, with the three-finger gesture, or pinned to the bar. In the bar's Show menu, "Settings button" replaces "Files button".
- **The power menu names your system.** On a Mac it says "Switch to the Mac desktop" and shows the desktop (the Dock brings Scorpion back), instead of the Windows wording. On Scorpion OS, which has no other desktop, it shows no such entry.
- **Workspaces, Preview and Plan stay visible** when several apps share the orb and the chat window is narrow: the top bar wraps instead of cutting them off.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
