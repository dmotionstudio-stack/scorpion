Scorpion 0.1.13 for Windows and macOS: type with your voice into any app, and hand gestures that keep up with your hand. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

Already on 0.1.12 for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.12

**Type what I say (Windows)**
- Whatever you say is typed into the app in front: an email, a document, a chat, a search box. Click where the words should go and press **Win+Alt+T** (or **Ctrl+Alt+T**), or put your thumb and little finger out with hand gestures on.
- A small pill at the bottom of the screen shows it's listening. Say "new line", "new paragraph", "delete that" to take back the last sentence, and "stop typing" to finish.
- It types in the language chosen in Settings → Voice, and nothing you say leaves the computer.

**Hand gestures keep up**
- The hand tracker runs on a thread of its own, so it no longer holds up the rest of Scorpion (with the orb open it used to block the page about half the time).
- While your hands are followed, the orb leaves room on the graphics chip: at most 30 frames a second and fewer pixels, so each hand frame is seen sooner.
- The orb's resolution follows your graphics chip by itself: lower when it can't keep up, higher again when it can.
- In dim light many webcams drop to 15 frames a second; Scorpion now keeps them at their full rate.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
