Scorpion 0.1.62 for Windows and Mac: wake the humanoid with two claps or a finger snap. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.62.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.62-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.62.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.61

- **Wake it with two claps or a finger snap.** With the orb open, two claps in a row, or one loud finger snap, wake it like saying its name: the humanoid sways its head hello, greets you and listens. Speech, fans, music, typing and long noises do not wake it, and one clap alone does not. Nothing is downloaded, and nothing is kept. It needs the voice installed. Say "turn off clapping" or "turn on finger snapping" to switch it.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
