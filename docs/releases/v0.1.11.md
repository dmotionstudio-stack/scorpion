Scorpion 0.1.11 for Windows and macOS: a new taskbar, your apps as stones on a glowing horizon. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion.Setup.0.1.11.exe` |

| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.11.dmg` |

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**). The README's Install section has the details.

Installing over 0.1.10 keeps your settings, chats and models.

## What's new since 0.1.10

**The Scorpion taskbar**
- Your apps rest as stones on a glowing horizon along the bottom of the screen, each with a live picture of its window set into the rock. The app in front is a large sapphire stone with its windows fanned inside and a ring of light under it.
- The stones float gently, a light hovers over each one, and dotted orbits link them. Underneath: **OPEN** (Scorpion), the time, battery, volume and network.
- **Click** a stone to bring its app forward, or to open it. Click the app in front for its card: its recent windows, or what to do with it.
- **Raise** a stone (drag it up) to defer its app: its windows step away. **Drop** it (drag it down) to focus: it comes forward and everything else steps away. Handwritten hints show you how the first few times.
- It sizes itself to your screen, lets clicks through its empty space to the apps underneath, and can hide until the pointer touches the bottom edge.
- It's the new default. To go back to the edge rail or the classic bar: **Settings → Windows and taskbar**, or right-click an empty part of the taskbar → **Layout**.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
