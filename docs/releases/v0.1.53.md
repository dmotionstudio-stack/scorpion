Scorpion 0.1.53 for Windows and macOS: the humanoid draws itself in when the orb opens, and its background lines can be turned down. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.53.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.53-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.53.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.52

- **A welcome you can see.** Every time the orb opens, the humanoid draws itself in from the bottom up with a line of light climbing its body, and the stars fade in behind it. Its spoken introduction now also plays at sign-in.
- **Background lines: Subtle, Full or Off** (**Settings → General → Orb look**). The constellation behind the humanoid is now Subtle by default (dimmer); Full brings back the old strength, Off removes it.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
