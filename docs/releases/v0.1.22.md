Scorpion 0.1.22 for Windows and macOS: a floating orb you can call by name, and chosen models that load as chosen. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.22.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.22-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.22.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.21

- **A floating orb.** While Scorpion's window is behind other windows, minimized or in the tray, a small orb floats over everything. Say "Scorpion": it wakes, greets you ("Good morning. How can I help?"), listens, and answers aloud, with what is said shown beside it. Click it to talk, drag it anywhere, right-click for Talk / Open Scorpion / Hide. It listens for its name only while it floats; speech is turned into text on your computer and nothing is kept. Needs the voice installed; turn it off in **Settings → Voice and hands → The orb**.
- **A model you choose loads as chosen.** In Light performance, Scorpion used to swap your chosen model for a faster one without asking. Now a model you pick by hand always loads; only Auto mode uses the faster one.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
