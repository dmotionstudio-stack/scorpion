Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.53

- **A livelier humanoid.** A soft, breathing glow in its face with blinking eye lights, branching veins down to a beating heart orb on its chest, and light that runs up its body while it speaks. Alone, it glances slowly around.
- **It answers your hands and voice.** Show an open palm and it sways its head hello, give a thumbs-up and it nods, say "goodbye" and it bows, and it turns to look at an app as it opens.
- **New looks** (**Settings → General → Orb look**): Humanoid style (Hologram, Glass or Wireframe) and Glow (Soft, Normal or Bright), changing at once.
- **Soft sounds.** A short cue when it starts listening and a gentle chime when it finishes thinking, with a switch to turn them off.
- **Lighter on slow computers.** If frames keep dropping, the humanoid turns off its bloom glow and then draws at fewer pixels by itself.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
