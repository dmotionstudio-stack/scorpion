Scorpion 0.1.61 for Windows and Mac: natural voices for French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Hindi and Haitian Creole, better listening, 38 languages, and the apps back around the orb. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.60

- **Natural voices for more languages.** French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Hindi are now spoken by Kokoro, the same natural voice model as English, instead of the small robotic ones, with nothing extra to download. Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Hindi each have several voices to choose from (Settings → Voice). Spoken French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese were listened to again in testing and came back word for word.
- **Haitian Creole sounds like Creole.** It is read with a French voice, as before (no voice exists for it), but now saying Creole sounds instead of applying French spelling. It still has a French accent.
- **Better listening** (Settings → Voice → Listening): Whisper Small, a one-time download of about 260 MB. On noisy French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese speech it makes about a quarter of the mistakes, and takes about three times as long to understand a sentence, so it is a choice.
- **38 languages.** Sixteen more (Greek, Hebrew, Thai, Bengali, Urdu, Persian, Czech, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Hungarian, Catalan, Tamil, Filipino, Malay), spoken by your computer's own voice when it has one for the language.
- **The apps are back around the orb.** The orb sphere shows its ring of apps again (Apps, Files, Library, Chats, People, Settings and your open windows). The humanoid has none.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
