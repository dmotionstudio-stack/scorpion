Scorpion 0.1.30 for Windows and macOS: the orb has its own conversation, Scorpion can run a Mac, a taskbar you arrange yourself, and a new 9B model. Everything still runs on your computer: the models, voice, faces and hand gestures never leave it.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Scorpion-Setup-0.1.30.exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) | `Scorpion-0.1.30-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Scorpion-0.1.30.dmg` |

Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.26

(0.1.27, 0.1.28 and 0.1.29 were never published: their Mac builds failed a test. This one has everything.)

- **The orb has its own conversation.** What you say or type to the orb goes to a conversation of its own (a hidden chat, not listed with your chats). Chats never show in the orb and are never read aloud while the orb is open (Read aloud speaks chats again once the orb is closed).
- **Scorpion can run your Mac (macOS).** The agent lists and opens your apps, lists and switches windows (focus, minimize, zoom, close), opens System Settings panes, and uses volume, dark mode, screenshot, lock, sleep and power controls. macOS asks once for Automation (System Events) and Accessibility access; until then Scorpion says exactly what to turn on.
- **A taskbar you arrange yourself (Windows, macOS and the orb).** Right-click an empty part of it: **Show** turns every button on or off (Scorpion, Apps, Files, Power, network, battery, volume, clock, notifications, mail, chat, USB drives, Orb settings). **Add a folder or file…** keeps folders and files on the bar (a folder opens in Files, a file in its program). Pinned apps and these can be moved left or right from their right-click menu.
- **The taskbar on a Mac.** The notification, network and battery icons open System Settings, Mail opens your mail program, apps and open windows show the Mac's own icons, and Files shows Mac paths (This Mac › Macintosh HD › …), the startup disk and the Trash. This was not run on a Mac by its maker: tell us what still does nothing.
- **New model: Qwen3.5 9B Defiant Fable** (6.3 GB, a community fine-tune) in the model list. Too big for the installers and the USB system, so it is a download.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
