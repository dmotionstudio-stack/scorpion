Already on 0.1.12 or newer for Windows? Scorpion downloads this version by itself and installs it when it restarts (**Settings → General → System → Updates → Check now** to look right away). On a Mac, Scorpion tells you it's out and **Download** opens it. Older versions (0.1.8 to 0.1.11) can't update themselves: install this one over them once, and your chats, models and settings stay.

The installers aren't signed with paid certificates. On Windows, click **More info → Run anyway** if SmartScreen stops it. On a Mac, open Scorpion once, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older: right-click Scorpion in Applications → **Open**).

## What's new since 0.1.24

- **Apps fit their tiles (Windows).** Scorpion asks each app for the smallest size it allows and makes its tile at least that big, so nothing is cut off; with four or more tiles, Automatic uses one row when the apps are too tall for two. Apps that draw their own title bar and minimize, maximize and close buttons (Calculator and other Store apps) have them hidden, so the only title bar and buttons are Scorpion's.
- **Resize from the top and bottom too.** The orb's tile has a layout button (▦): automatic, side by side, one above the other, or a grid of two rows. Tiles above each other are resized by dragging the bar between them up or down. Also in **Settings → General → Orb**.
- **Conversation text follows the orb.** What is said and answered sits inside the orb's tile, below or above the orb (or hidden): **Settings → General → Orb → Conversation text**.
- **The title can move.** S.C.O.R.P.I.O.N. can be at the top left, center or right, or hidden: drag it, or choose in **Settings → General → Orb → Title**.
- **The taskbar in the orb:** the intent wheel button is gone. Its **Show** menu (right-click it) can now hide the USB drives icon and the Orb settings button, and the grouped status dropdown lists every icon in it, USB drives included.

Scorpion OS (the Linux system you can boot from a USB stick) and the Linux packages come from the **Scorpion OS** workflow, not this release.
