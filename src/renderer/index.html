Library

People

Scorpion can recognize the people you add here. In Interact it knows who is talking, the agent can find them in your photos, and face lock opens Scorpion only for its owner. Faces are kept as face prints on this computer only; your photos are never copied or uploaded.

Add a person

Use 2–5 clear photos of the face, looking at the camera, in good light. More photos (different days, glasses or not) make recognition more reliable.

Face insights

Ported from DeepFace: what a face shows (expression, apparent age and gender) and whether it is a real face or a photo of one. The agent can use these on your photos ("how old does she look here?", "are these two photos the same person?"), Interact shows them under each face, and face lock refuses photos and screens. Optional downloads; everything runs on this computer. Age and gender are guesses from appearance.

Face lock

When it's on, Scorpion opens locked and only unlocks when it sees the owner's face (or you type your PIN). It keeps other people out of the app; it does not encrypt your files. Without the liveness check, a printed photo of the owner might fool it.

Off: Scorpion never uses the network while you chat. On: the agent can look things up (DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia) and read pages for current information, and names its sources. Your chats, files and voice still stay on this computer.

While gestures are on, the camera runs whenever Scorpion is open (even behind other windows) and rests while Scorpion is minimized; on a Mac, only while the orb is open. Your hand isn't shown on screen, only a small status line. The video stays on this computer and is never recorded. With no hand in view it checks less often, to save power.

Balanced draws the orb at a calmer frame rate, checks the taskbar's windows less often and looks at fewer camera frames for hand tracking. Light goes further: the orb in lower detail, background media work (Library, faces) on fewer cores, and chats with the fastest model installed here when your chosen one is slow on this computer. No model is removed; Full brings your chosen model back.

The settings above are for the constellation map. Projects are constellations: their apps, folders, documents and websites orbit them. Click an item to open it, the star to resume the project, drag one item onto another to connect them, or anywhere to pin it there. Scroll out for the galaxy of all projects; type to search; List shows the same as plain lists. Shortcut examples: Super+Space, Super+Alt+Space, Control+Shift+Space (Windows keeps Win+Space for keyboard layouts; then Win+Alt+Space is used).

To leave full screen: Ctrl+Esc, or press Esc twice quickly. F11 goes back to full screen. Apps you open from Scorpion show inside the orb, on top of it. Windows itself (Ctrl+Alt+Delete, sign-in, its own alerts) can still come in front.

Open the orb: Scorpion starts with the orb filling the screen, greets you by name when face recognition is installed, and waits until you speak to it (or say "Scorpion" with the wake word on).
