Three ways to start

Try it, carry it, or install it.

Nothing installed

Try it from the stick

Restart, pick the stick in the boot menu, and choose Try or install Scorpion OS. Nothing on the computer changes, and nothing is kept when you turn it off.

Your AI in your pocket

Keep your changes on it

Choose the entry that keeps changes, and your chats, memories, models, Wi-Fi and settings stay on the stick from one computer to the next.

Alone or beside Windows

Install it

Click Install on the rail, or say "Install Scorpion OS". It installs alone or alongside Windows, with a menu to choose each time you start.

What's inside

A complete system, built around Scorpion.

Ubuntu 24.04 LTS underneath, with its kernel, drivers and firmware for Wi-Fi, Ethernet, sound, Bluetooth, graphics and printers.

Ready offline

A model (Qwen3 4B) and the voice are built in, so Scorpion chats and talks from the first minute with no internet.

Scorpion is the desktop

The orb, the edge rail, the intent wheel, Stage and Ctrl+K are the whole interface, with a spoken first start.

Updates that just work

Signed updates from Scorpion's own repository. It tells you when they're ready and installs them with progress.

Undo a change

System snapshots before changes, so a bad update or install can be put back. Your files are never touched.

Get more apps

Ask for an app, or search for one, and Scorpion installs it from Flathub for you, without a password.

Quick settings

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, volume, brightness and battery beside the rail, and the agent can diagnose sound or network problems.

Accessible

Bigger text, the Orca screen reader and an on-screen keyboard, offered right after hello.

Secure Boot friendly

It boots through Ubuntu's signed loader on BIOS and UEFI computers, and runs in VirtualBox too.

Private by design

Same as the app: your chats, voice and camera never leave the computer.
