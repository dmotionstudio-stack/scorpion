What it does

Out of the box, on your computer.

Chat with local models

Streaming answers with Markdown, from Qwen, Llama, Gemma or any GGUF file you bring.

An agent that does the work

Reads, writes and edits files and runs commands in the folder you choose. It asks before touching anything else.

Talk to it

Hands-free conversation with Whisper and Kokoro voices, on your computer. Interrupt any time.

Runs your computer

Opens apps and settings, handles windows, volume, Wi-Fi and power, by typing or voice. Anything that changes something asks first.

Specialist agents

Coder, Researcher, Writer, Data Analyst, File Organizer and Reviewer, working together on bigger jobs.

Scheduled agents

Run an agent every morning, on a timer, or when files change. Reports arrive as notifications.

Chat with documents

Add PDFs, Word and text files. Scorpion indexes them locally and names its sources.

Memory across chats

It remembers your name, projects and preferences, and recalls earlier conversations. You can view or erase it all.

Face lock and people

Optional face recognition greets people by name, sorts photos by person, and locks Scorpion to you.

A desktop built around your AI

Everything is one gesture away.

New in 0.1.8: Scorpion can take over the desktop itself, with a few new ways to get anywhere fast.

Summon dot and intent wheel

A glowing dot stays on your desktop. Click it, or press Win+Shift+Space, and a glass wheel offers Write, Browse, Talk, Create, Files and Focus.

Stage

Touch the left edge and every open app slides in as a live picture. Pick one, and the window in front steps aside.

Command palette

Ctrl+K finds everything Scorpion can do. Type it, say it ("open Wi-Fi settings"), or start typing on the wheel.

Edge rail and taskbar

A glass rail down the right edge with the clock, battery, notifications and your apps. It can hide until you touch the edge.

Constellations

Each project is a star with its apps, files and websites in orbit. Click the star to pick up right where you left off.

Performance switch

Full, Balanced or Light, and Light automatically on battery, so Scorpion stays quick on older PCs and laptops.

Private by design

Nothing you say leaves your computer.

The model, your chats, your memories and your files all stay on your machine. Online search is off until you turn it on, and the agent asks before it touches folders outside your workspace.

  • No account and no sign-in, ever
  • Changes and commands wait for your approval
  • System folders stay read-only, even with full access
  • Camera and microphone are processed on your computer and never recorded
