The humanoid

The orb can become a humanoid.

Scorpion can show itself as a holographic orb or as a living humanoid, and change between the two when you ask. It listens, thinks and speaks, follows your hand and face, and reacts. Move your pointer over it.

  • It enters in a different way each time: from its heart, dissolving, a spiral, a spin, a jelly pop, a fly-in, or growing from a seed of light
  • It sways its head hello to an open palm, nods to a thumbs-up, and bows when you say goodbye
  • Say "transform into the humanoid" or "show me the orb", and it changes in slow motion
  • It waits until you have finished, and listens for your voice, not the room

What the orb tells you

You always know what Scorpion is doing.

Standby

Calm and slow. Say "Scorpion", press Space, or give a thumbs up to start talking.

Listening

The core pulses with your voice while Whisper turns speech into text on your computer. It waits until you have finished, and hears your voice, not the fans and keyboards.

Thinking

The shells spin faster and the code around it races while the model works out an answer.

Working

A tool is running: opening an app, reading files, changing a setting. The scan rings sweep.

Asking you

It holds still and waits. Answer out loud, click, or give a thumbs up or down.

Speaking

It glows with its own voice, and starts the first sentence as soon as it is ready. Interrupt any time with Space.

The orb as the system

Run your whole computer from the orb.

Apps inside the orb

Every app you open gets a tile beside the orb, up to six at once, like Windows Snap, and the orb stays on screen. Leave the orb and every app gets its normal window back.

Ask out loud

"Switch to Outlook", "open Wi-Fi settings", "lock the screen", "turn off hand gestures", "what's that noise?". Anything that changes something is asked out loud first.

The humanoid

A second form of the orb: a 3D hologram figure that follows your hand and face, listens, speaks and reacts. "Transform into the humanoid" and "show me the orb" switch between them, in slow motion.

Looks you can change

Hologram, Glass or Wireframe, a soft, normal or bright glow, background lines on or off, and soft sounds when it starts listening and finishes thinking.

It knows you

With face recognition on, it turns toward whoever is talking and greets them by name.

Gentle alerts

It pulses for low battery, finished downloads, schedules and check-ins, and can say them aloud.

The mini orb

Minimize it to a small living orb that floats over everything. Click it to come back.
