What the orb tells you

You always know what Scorpion is doing.

Standby

Calm and slow. Say "Scorpion", press Space, or give a thumbs up to start talking.

Listening

The core pulses with your voice while Whisper turns speech into text on your computer.

Thinking

The shells spin faster and the code around it races while the model works out an answer.

Working

A tool is running: opening an app, reading files, changing a setting. The scan rings sweep.

Asking you

It holds still and waits. Answer out loud, click, or give a thumbs up or down.

Speaking

It glows with its own voice. Interrupt any time with Space.

Apps inside the orb

The app you bring forward fills a panel beside the orb. Leave the orb and every app gets its normal window back.

Ask out loud

"Switch to Outlook", "open Wi-Fi settings", "lock the screen". Anything that changes something is asked out loud first.

Constellations

An optional look: each project is a star with its apps, files and websites in orbit. Click the star to resume it.

It knows you

With face recognition on, it turns toward whoever is talking and greets them by name.

Gentle alerts

It pulses for low battery, finished downloads, schedules and check-ins, and can say them aloud.
