Questions

Before you order

Will it work on my computer?

Scorpion OS starts on almost any 64-bit PC, with Secure Boot on or off. Scorpion itself runs on Windows 10 and 11 and on Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel. 8 GB of memory is enough for the built-in model.

Does it change my computer?

No. Booting Scorpion OS from the stick and running Scorpion from it leave the computer as it was. You can install Scorpion OS to the computer's disk if you want to, but that is your choice.

Do I need internet?

No. The model and the voice are on the stick. Online mode is off until you turn it on.

Which size should I get?

The 64 GB stick has Scorpion OS, Scorpion for Windows and Mac, Qwen3 4B and the voice. The 128 GB stick adds all six tested models and plenty of room for your own chats, models and documents.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5 to 10 business days and express 2 to 4. You choose on the secure payment page, where you also enter your address.

What's the difference between booting it and running it?

Booting starts Scorpion OS from the stick: the computer restarts into Scorpion and nothing is changed on its disk. Running starts the Scorpion app from the stick while Windows or macOS is already open, and writes nothing to the computer. Both keep your data on the stick.

Can I also download Scorpion?

Yes. The Complete and Lifetime editions include every installer and Scorpion OS. See Pricing. You can add a stick to any edition at checkout.
