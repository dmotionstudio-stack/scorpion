Scorpion on a USB stick
=======================

An offline AI assistant that runs straight from this stick on Windows and Mac.
Nothing is installed, and no internet is needed. Your chats, memories, settings
and models are all saved on the stick in the "scorpion-data" folder, and both
Windows and Mac use the same folder.

What's on the stick
  Windows\Scorpion.exe          Scorpion for Windows 10 and 11
  Mac\...Apple Silicon Mac.dmg  Scorpion for Macs with an M1, M2, M3 or M4 chip
  Mac\...Intel Mac.dmg          Scorpion for Macs with an Intel processor
  scorpion-data\                Your data and models (keep this folder here)


WINDOWS
-------
1. Open the Windows folder and double-click Scorpion.exe.
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then
   Run anyway. This appears because Scorpion isn't signed with a paid
   certificate.
3. Scorpion opens with the model on the stick already selected.


MAC
---
1. Open the Mac folder and double-click the .dmg for your Mac.
   (Apple menu > About This Mac: "Chip" means Apple Silicon, "Processor"
   means Intel.)
2. In the window that opens, double-click Scorpion. Don't drag it to
   Applications: run it from here so it uses the data on the stick.
3. If macOS asks to let Scorpion access files on a removable volume, click
   Allow. Without this, Scorpion can't read the stick.
4. If macOS says "Apple could not verify Scorpion", click Done, then open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, and open
   Scorpion again.
5. When you're done, quit Scorpion, then eject the Scorpion disk image and
   the stick.


TIPS
----
- Quit Scorpion before you unplug the stick, so nothing is lost.
- Models: Settings > Models in Scorpion. Models you download there are saved
  on the stick and work on every computer.
- Big models: if the stick is formatted FAT32, files over 4 GB can't be
  saved. Format it as exFAT (works on both Windows and Mac) for bigger models.
- A faster stick (USB 3) makes models load much faster.
- To start fresh, delete everything inside scorpion-data except the models
  folder.
